ABOUT

Connecting brands, entertainment, culture and media from China.

ArtSoul Culture operates through three business divisions: Brand, Entertainment, and Culture & Media.

Who We Are

ArtSoul Culture is a China-based culture and creative company serving brands, artists, actors, creators, media platforms and cultural projects.

The company holds a valid Business Performance Permit in China and is qualified to provide performance and artist-representation services.

We assess project objectives, audience profile, content positioning, commercial terms and delivery risk, then manage the process from strategy and rights confirmation through production and final delivery.
